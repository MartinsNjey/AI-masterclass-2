# Kisumu Fish Market – Q1 2024 Findings Report

## 1. Top Revenue Vendors
Otieno generated the highest revenue at KES 173,100, followed by Kamau at KES 109,935 and Achieng at KES 103,175. These three vendors are the strongest revenue contributors in the dataset.

## 2. Retail vs Wholesale Performance
Retail transactions achieved an average selling price of KES 502.40 per kg, compared with KES 459.40 per kg for wholesale transactions. Retail therefore generated KES 43 more per kg on average.

## 3. Monthly Revenue Trend
Total revenue increased from KES 1,121,180 in January to KES 1,391,150 in February, reaching KES 2,367,730 in March. This indicates a strong upward revenue trend during the first quarter of 2024.

## Recommendation
The association should support the three highest-performing vendors while developing strategies to increase retail sales. It should also investigate the factors behind March's strong performance and identify ways to sustain that growth.
