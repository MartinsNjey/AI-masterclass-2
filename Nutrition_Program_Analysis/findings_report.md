## Finding 1: County performance

**Expectation:** We expected all three counties to show positive weight-gain results.

**What happened:** Kisumu recorded the highest average weight gain, followed by Nakuru and Nairobi.

**Why it matters:** The NGO can investigate what is working well in Kisumu and whether those practices can improve results in the other counties.

## Finding 2: Attendance and Weight Gain

**Expectation:** We expected participants who attended more sessions to achieve better weight-gain results.

**What happened:** The data showed a very strong positive relationship between sessions attended and weight gain, with a correlation of 0.987.

**Why it matters:** Encouraging regular attendance may help improve nutrition program outcomes, although the data does not prove that attendance alone causes weight gain.

## Finding 3: Age Group Performance

**Expectation:** We expected weight-gain results to differ between participant age groups.

**What happened:** Participants aged 0–5 recorded the highest average weight gain at 3.70 kg, while those aged 13–17 recorded the lowest at 2.77 kg.

**Why it matters:** The NGO should examine whether different age groups need different nutrition support to improve program outcomes.

## Presentation Practice — Questions and Answers

**1. Why should the NGO pay attention to Kisumu's results?**

The NGO should identify the successful practices used in Kisumu and consider applying them in Nairobi and Nakuru to improve weight-gain outcomes.

**2. What does attendance tell us about program outcomes?**

Participants who attend more nutrition sessions tend to achieve greater weight gain, suggesting that regular attendance is linked to better program outcomes.

**3. What should the NGO consider doing for older participants?**

The NGO should investigate whether older participants need different nutrition support or a more suitable program approach to improve their outcomes.

## Questions the Data Cannot Answer

The current dataset provides useful information about county performance, attendance and age-group outcomes. However, it does not answer every question the NGO or donor might ask.

1. **Why did Kisumu perform better?** The dataset does not explain which specific nutrition practices contributed to its higher average weight gain.

2. **Why did some participants miss sessions?** We do not have information about barriers such as transport costs, availability or personal circumstances.

3. **Why did older participants gain less weight?** The dataset does not include detailed dietary information or individual nutritional needs.

4. **Were participants healthy before joining the program?** We do not have their starting health assessments or baseline nutritional status.

5. **Did the program cause the weight gain?** The data shows relationships but does not establish that the program alone caused the outcomes.
